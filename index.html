What it does

A researcher, not an autocomplete.

Every feature below exists because a market leader gets it wrong. We read the reviews so you don't have to live in them.

Deep research

It reads Reddit for you

Every route triggers real web searches across Reddit threads, forums and trip reports — segment by segment. The stops that come back are the ones humans got excited about, not the ones with an ad budget.

Storybooks

Every stop is a storybook

Tap any stop and it opens as its own page — a photo gallery, the food travelers rave about, adventures, places to crash, and live prices. Every route researched teaches the library a new storybook, forever.

Receipts

Every claim links to its source

Tap the chip on any stop and you land on the actual thread. A full research log shows every query we ran and every page we read. No black box.

Hidden gems

A score for how secret it is

One flame means everyone stops there. Five means you'd only know from a local or a buried comment. Sort your trip by how far off the tourist trail you actually want to be.

Day logic

Days that split themselves

Set one slider — max hours behind the wheel — and the trip breaks into days automatically. All of the power planners' logic, none of their config screens.

Navigation

One tap into Google Maps

We do the research and day-splitting; your nav app does the turns. Each day exports as a ready-made route, chunked to never hit a waypoint cap.

Big rigs

RV mode, honestly labeled

Biases every suggestion toward big-vehicle parking and access, and flags a clearance advisory on each stop. An advisory — we'll tell you plainly when it isn't certified routing.

Control

Your stops outrank ours

Add, reorder, or delete anything. The research proposes; you decide. Your hand-added stops are marked as yours and never overwritten.

Memory

Trips that survive the drive

Save a route and it's still there next season — with the sources, the order, and the stops you actually loved. Plans shouldn't evaporate at the destination.

The rule

AI that never makes up your numbers.

Every drive time, detour, and day total is either computed, retrieved, or shown as a question mark. Our researcher can dig, argue for a stop, and tell you what a thread said — but it is structurally forbidden from inventing a single figure, hour, or price. When it doesn't know, it says so. That's the rule, and it's the only reason to trust a planner at all.

EST = model estimate, labeled ? = unknown, never guessed No invented hours or prices No source = flagged unverified
