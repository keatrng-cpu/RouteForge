Design ledger — every market weakness, and the fix shipped here
Roadtrippers: cut free users from ~27 waypoints to 7; billing dark patterns bred rage-churn.
Fix: unlimited stops, no account, no billing surface at all. Nothing can auto-renew.
Roadtrippers: stale POIs — closed restaurants, wrong hours.
Fix: optional live web-verify pass on every generation; freshness is a runtime check, not a database rotting on a shelf.
Copilot2trip / AI planners: hallucinated travel times, unreliable picks, features the model didn't know it had.
Enhance (the LuxeForge doctrine): the model is contractually forbidden from inventing numbers — unknown legs render as ?, every stop carries a confidence gauge and a verify link. Structurally honest, not vibes-honest.
Google Maps: no along-the-route discovery, ~10-waypoint cap, plans evaporate after the drive.
Fix: we own discovery + day logic + persistence, then export each day as a Maps link under the cap. Complement the giant, don't fight it.
Furkot: deepest day-logic in the category, buried under a brutal learning curve.
Enhance: the same auto day-splitting reduced to one slider — max hours per day. Zero config screens.
Wanderlog: collaboration is the growth loop, but premium features stay locked for everyone else in the group.
Fix: share codes are free for all collaborators. The viral loop is never taxed.
RV Life: proves people pay for fear-removal (bridge strikes) — but general planners ignore RVs entirely.
Fix (honest v1): RV mode biases suggestions toward big-rig-friendly stops and flags a clearance/parking advisory on every stop. Advisory, not certified routing — labeled as such.
Autio: great story layer, thin coverage off marquee corridors.
Enhance: every suggested stop ships with a one-line "why it's worth it," generated for anywhere — labeled color, not fact.
TripStone / MYV / WilderTrips: no accumulating asset — nothing compounds.
Fix: saved trips + share codes are the seed of a proprietary trip-corpus; the data layer is designed to compound from day one.
